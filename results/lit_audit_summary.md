### 关键原文证据

- **Weiss 2015, Drug Alcohol Depend (cannabis use)**（PMID 26422462）："NHANES does not validate the response with a review of the medical record or perform diagnostic testing relevant to IBD"
- **de Silva 2017, Environ Pollut (urinary phenols and IBD)**（PMID 28689150）："The study database utilized does not allow us to obtain confirmatory medical records ... and we relied on self reported outcomes. ... The prevalence of IBD was limited to those respondents who completed the supplemental questionnaire on arthritis and was not a chronic disease that is directly queried in the main survey leading to a likely underestimation of disease prevalence"
- **Gut Liver 2017 (IBD and depressive symptoms)**（PMID 28395506）："the study was based on self-reported history of IBD and DS, which can be subject to recall bias"
- **J Ophthalmic Inflamm Infect 2018 (uveitis)**（PMID 29666980）："recall bias may influence the results and add more uncertainty to the prevalence estimate ... There is no evidence in the literature of the validity of self-reported uveitis for epidemiologic studies"
- **Biol Trace Elem Res 2021 (copper/zinc and IBD)**（PMID 32939644）："Our analysis has potential limitations. The cross-sectional design of the study is a major limitation"
- **Biol Trace Elem Res 2021 (iron and IBD)**（PMID 33409918）："Causal inference between iron intake and chronic diarrhea cannot be made because of the cross-sectional design of NHANES ... residual confounding by other unmeasured factors cannot be excluded"
- **Front Neurol 2022 (sleep quality and IBD)**（PMID 36090851）："the assessments of bowel health and sleep quality were based on self-reports, which might not be accurate but serves as the only conventional way in large epidemiological investigations"
- **Mayo Clin Proc Innov 2022 (axial pain in IBD)**（PMID 36147868）："these self-reports are generally consistent with actual physician-diagnosed IBD. Cases of IBD from NHANES 2009-2010 had high colonoscopy rates at diagnosis, increased rates of characteristic IBD gastrointestinal symptoms, and IBD-related extraintestinal manifestations"
- **Weisman 2023, Prev Med Rep**（PMID 37223580）："no clinical records were available, so we cannot validate the self-reported medically diagnosed IBD estimates. The extent of possible misdiagnosis cannot be determined"
- **Front Nutr 2023 (ulcerative colitis and PSA)**（PMID 38111604）："it is probable that information bias such as information on the UC and AGE that was assessed through a questionnaire, which could affect the association, was observed"
- **Clin Exp Pharmacol Physiol 2024 (dietary inflammatory index)**（PMID 38643975）："The NHANES database diagnoses IBD based on individual reports of study participants"
- **Clin Res Hepatol Gastroenterol 2024 (phenols and UC)**（PMID 39313067）："This is likely attributed to the limited number of UC cases in our sample and the inclusion of too many confounders"
- **Sci Rep 2024 (visceral adiposity index and bowel habits)**（PMID 39397029）："the information collected may have been influenced by participants' subjective awareness ... because of the small number of IBD patients in the NHANES database and the relatively inadequate sample size included, caution should be exercised in interpreting IBD results"
- **Asia Pac J Clin Nutr 2024 (EPA/DHA and IBD)**（PMID 39209366）："the NHANES determined the presence of IBD based on a questionnaire, and did not confirm this diagnosis with an endoscopic procedure, thereby likely leading to some misreported data. In the future, endoscopic pathology should be used as a diagnostic method"
- **Nutrition 2025 (nutrient intake and IBD, dose-response)**（PMID 39700661）："Another minor limitation is that our confirmation of IBD relies solely on the NHANES 2009-2010 survey questionnaire ... Some cases may be misdiagnosed or underreported, potentially affecting the accuracy of our assessment of IBD incidence"
- **BMC Gastroenterol 2025 (vitamins C, D, K and IBD)**（PMID 40097943）："IBD status was determined based on one self-reported question from the participants, which is subject to potential bias. Future studies may consider using physician diagnosis or other more objective tools for IBD assessment"
- **Front Physiol 2025 (non-HDL/HDL ratio and bowel health)**（PMID 40342858）："The reliance on self-reported data could also lead to inaccuracies in assessing gut health status"
- **J Multidiscip Healthc 2025 (caffeine and IBD)**（PMID 40600201）："bowel habits were defined by participants' self-reports, and the information collected may be influenced by participants' subjective awareness"
- **Acta Psychol 2026 (IBD and anxiety/depression)**（PMID 42184804）："IBD status was based on self-reported physician diagnosis and could not be verified against medical records, and Crohn's disease and ulcerative colitis could not be reliably differentiated"

## 结论（可写入稿件）

- 16/19 篇全文在文字上承认自报条目有问题；**3 篇连提都未提**（局限段只谈横断面设计与混杂）；**0/19 篇把该问题转化为任何定量处理**；**0/19 篇换用更严格定义重跑主分析**。
- 2/19 篇反向主张该条目可靠，依据是结肠镜率与肠外表现——正是本研究定量检验的那类论证。
- **1 篇仅摘要的边界**：把它们的五个维度按最坏情况填满，提及率也不过从 16/19 变成 17/20；反向主张可靠从 2 篇升到 3 篇——方向与量级都不变。故「承认但未量化」这一结论不受全文缺失影响。
- 12 篇有需要单独征引的细节，分 5 类：
  - **选择性怀疑** —— 同一套词汇用在结局上，不用在自己的暴露上
    - **J Ophthalmic Inflamm Infect 2018 (uveitis)**（PMID 29666980）：reasons about sensitivity/specificity and direction for its OUTCOME (self-reported uveitis), then reports OR 11.3 and 22.2 for self-reported UC/CD as predictors without applying the same reasoning to its exposure
    - **Nutrition 2025 (nutrient intake and IBD, dose-response)**（PMID 39700661）：devotes a full paragraph to remediating dietary recall -- participant training, food frequency questionnaires, biomarkers, mobile apps -- while the disease-status item is filed under "another minor limitation"; the same self-report vocabulary, inverted priorities. Also calls a cross-sectional count "incidence"
  - **可信度误挂** —— 用只处理混杂的方法，去担保测量误差
    - **de Silva 2017, Environ Pollut (urinary phenols and IBD)**（PMID 28689150）：the same Discussion that admits reliance on self-reported outcomes then declares "Strengths of our study included the use of a well validated survey tool", without excluding the one item that was neither directly queried nor validated. Second report (after Zhu 2024) to locate the item in the arthritis supplemental questionnaire; claims the item likely underestimates prevalence without quantification. Source is a scanned PDF with no text layer; passages transcribed from page images (data/fulltext/28689150_pdf.txt)
    - **Front Nutr 2023 (ulcerative colitis and PSA)**（PMID 38111604）：adds a two-sample Mendelian randomisation and concludes that "the observations are more credible and reliable"; the MR addresses confounding, not the questionnaire exposure, so the credibility claim is attached to the wrong bias
  - **报道口径** —— 摘要的样本量与真正支撑该估计的样本量不一致
    - **Sci Rep 2024 (visceral adiposity index and bowel habits)**（PMID 39397029）：states that the IBD question was fielded only in 2009–2010 and restricts the analysis to 4,535 participants — correctly — but the abstract reports the 10,391-participant cohort, so the exposure-specific sample is invisible to anyone reading only the abstract
    - **Asia Pac J Clin Nutr 2024 (EPA/DHA and IBD)**（PMID 39209366）：the most explicit statement in the set, and still no correction: the paper names the missing endoscopy, then reports quartile odds ratios as if the exposure were measured without error. It also calls the outcome "incidence" in 12 places and "prevalence" in 7 in a cross-sectional design, and attaches a direction ("may underestimate the prevalence") to the dietary limitation rather than to the diagnostic one
  - **条目溯源** —— 正文给出的变量名对不上该周期真实存在的条目
    - **Acta Psychol 2026 (IBD and anxiety/depression)**（PMID 42184804）：the Methods name MCQ160N and MCQ160O as the Crohn's and ulcerative colitis items. In the 2009-2010 cycle IBD is carried by ARQ125C and ARQ125D; MCQ160N asks about gout and MCQ160O is not fielded at all (MCQ_F holds 81 variables, none IBD-related). The reported case count, 56 of 6,398 (0.9%), matches an ARQ-based definition rather than the 4.5% gout item, so the analysis itself is probably unaffected -- but a reader cannot reconstruct which variable produced the exposure, and the paper calls the item "physician-diagnosed" in the same sentence that concedes it was never verified
  - **其他** —— 细节值得征引，但不足以单独立类
    - **Weiss 2015, Drug Alcohol Depend (cannabis use)**（PMID 26422462）：the earliest user of the item, and the only one that states outright in Methods that the response is never validated against records. It even reports the colonoscopy rates (UC 73.4%, CD 96.5%) as descriptive detail but never uses them to argue validity or adjust anything; the limitation paragraph is spent on causality, not measurement. The abstract writes weighted counts as "2084,895 subjects with IBD" and calls a cross-sectional prevalence "incidence"
    - **Biol Trace Elem Res 2021 (copper/zinc and IBD)**（PMID 32939644）：the entire limitation discussion is one sentence about cross-sectional design; 56 IBD cases, weighted prevalence 1.14%, and the measurement of the case definition is never raised
    - **Biol Trace Elem Res 2021 (iron and IBD)**（PMID 33409918）：despite the 2007-2010 span in its title, the IBD analysis is correctly restricted to the 2009-2010 cycle (47 cases of 4,043) while chronic diarrheal symptoms use the full span -- the honest way to handle the item's availability. The limitation list covers design and confounding only
    - **Clin Exp Pharmacol Physiol 2024 (dietary inflammatory index)**（PMID 38643975）：uses the self-report fact as an interpretive lever -- patients may have changed their diets after diagnosis -- rather than as a measurement problem to be assessed; one of the two reports besides ours to locate the IBD questions correctly, as "a sub-analysis of the Arthritis Questionnaire" (the other is de Silva 2017)
    - **Clin Res Hepatol Gastroenterol 2024 (phenols and UC)**（PMID 39313067）：attributes attenuated associations to case numbers and confounder count but never questions the UC label itself; covariates are described as self-reported throughout, the outcome never is
- 准确表述是：16/19 篇「承认但未量化」（acknowledged but not analysed），3 篇「只字未提」，**没有任何一篇做过定量处理**。上一版 0/20 与 11/11 的说法均已废弃。
